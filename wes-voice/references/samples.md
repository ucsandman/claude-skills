## What the numbers say

- Jokes and reactions get the most likes (the "robot slave" post, 10 likes, 240 impressions).
- Demos get the bookmarks (SideTap texting Dad, 3 bookmarks, 419 impressions). Bookmarks are the signal that someone wants to come back and use the thing.
- The event-plus-tool posts get replies (agent bought a URL, 5 likes, 128 impressions; declick launch, 2 replies).
- Product-description posts with no event in them get nothing (the "AI agents keep paying to rediscover interfaces" post, 0 likes).
- Long thoughtful posts do fine but not better than a one-line demo (the $25k contract post, 2 likes, 104 impressions; the identity post, 2 likes, 222 impressions).

So: a real thing that happened, shown, with the tool named in passing, beats a paragraph about the tool.

## Voice notes from the samples

- Lowercase openers happen ("got a ton of hate", "new record for"), but most posts start capitalized. Either is fine, don't force it.
- Profanity and "lol" show up in reactions, not in product posts.
- Wes names the tool in the middle of a sentence about what happened, never as the first word.
- "Pretty insane moment", "kinda neat", "not sure it's helpful" is the register for excitement. Understated, then the detail carries it.
- Hashtags appear on launch and demo posts only.
